# Tiny Desk AI

[Tiny Desk AI](https://tiny-desk.pro) is a web-based AI video generator that turns a single photo into a 15-second "tiny desk concert" video — the format behind the viral Tiny Desk AI trend on TikTok.

## What it does

Upload one clear photo of a person and the generator renders them performing at the tiny desk: a 15-second, 9:16 vertical MP4 with no watermark, ready for TikTok, Reels and Shorts. There is no prompt to write and no reference video to hunt for — the desk, the mic and the camera moves are pre-built into the template.

## Highlights

- **One photo in, one concert out** — the render keeps the face, hair and outfit from the photo, so the performer is unmistakably them
- **No prompt, no watermark** — upload a photo and press generate
- **Private by default** — photos are deleted automatically 7 days after upload, finished videos 30 days after rendering, and nothing is ever used to train AI models
- **Simple pricing** — flat credit packs from $5.99, credits never expire, no subscription, and a failed render is refunded automatically

## How it works

1. Upload one front-facing photo (JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 10 MB).
2. Confirm the consent box and press generate.
3. Download the watermark-free MP4 a few minutes later.
